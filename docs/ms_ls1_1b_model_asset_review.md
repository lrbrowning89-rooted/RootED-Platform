## MS-LS1-1B_model_brick_wall_cells_01

**SVG filename:** `ms-ls1-1b-brick-wall-cells-01.svg`

**Question ID:** `Q_MSLS1_1_B_019`

**Question:**

A model compares a brick wall to a living tissue. The bricks represent cells. What is one limitation of this model?

A. Bricks are not alive and do not perform life functions like cells do.  
B. The model shows that tissues can be made of smaller units.  
C. The model helps explain that cells can form larger structures.  
D. The model uses parts to represent a whole.

**Correct answer:** A

**Why the model supports the question:**  
The SVG directly compares a wall made of bricks with tissue made of cells, matching the analogy in the question. The caption note that cells are living parts while bricks are nonliving parts supports the idea of model limitations.

**Potential confusion or improvement:**  
The analogy is clear, but the tissue panel could more explicitly label the cell group as "living cells" to reinforce why answer A is the limitation.

**Question:**

A student claims, "This diagram proves that an entire organism is made of one giant cell." The diagram shows many small cells grouped together to form tissue. What is the best response?

A. The claim is correct because tissues are always one giant cell.

**Question:**

A diagram shows a seedling growing over several days. A second diagram zooms in to show cells dividing in the root tip. Which explanation best connects the diagrams to cell theory?

A. The seedling grows because new cells come from existing cells.  
B. The seedling grows because cells disappear over time.

D. The root tip is made of crystals, not cells.

## MS-LS1-1B_model_unknown_samples_01

**Question ID:** `Q_MSLS1_1_B_015`

**Question:**

A scientist studies several unknown samples. Sample 1 has repeating cell-like structures under a microscope. Sample 2 has crystals with sharp edges. Sample 3 has cells dividing into two cells. Which samples provide evidence related to cell theory?

A. Samples 1 and 3  
B. Samples 1 and 2  
C. Samples 2 and 3  
D. Samples 1, 2, and 3

**Correct answer:** A

**Why the model supports the question:**  
The SVG contrasts a sample with visible cells against a sample without visible cells, reinforcing that cell-like structures are relevant evidence and crystals are not. This helps students eliminate answer choices that include Sample 2.

**Potential confusion or improvement:**
